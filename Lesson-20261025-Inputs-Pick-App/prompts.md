## 13. First Environment Proposal

```text
Propose a funny Environment
```

Produced option: **"Corporate Fishing"**

An office flooded after someone tried to "sync everything to the cloud."

- Fisherman: An exhausted intern on a floating office chair, casting with a mouse cable.
- Fish: Swimming staplers, wild keyboards, coffee mugs, and elusive bonus envelopes.
- Big catch: A shark in a business suit named "Upper Management."
- Bait: A dangling USB stick labeled `salary_list.xlsx`.
- Escapes: "This meeting could have been an email!" as your catch slips away.
- Background: Printer islands, monitor reefs, and a rubber duck running stand-up.
- Sound: Keyboard clicks, notification pings, and a triumphant Windows-style chime on each catch.

Same fishing physics - just another day at the office.

## 14. A Totally Different Environment

```text
Totally different environment. Currently there are two of them, propose a funny ingenious different one
```

Produced option: **"The Great Sock Dimension"**

The secret universe inside a washing machine - where every missing sock goes.

- Environment: A swirling galaxy of soap bubbles, lint asteroids, and tiny planets made of tangled laundry. A giant washing-machine window frames the scene.
- Fisherman: A garden gnome riding a rubber duck, wielding a bent coat hanger with a clothespin hook.
- Your catches: Shy ankle socks, feral underwear, jellyfish bras, and a majestic fitted sheet that moves like a manta ray.
- The shark equivalent: A furious red sock that turns nearby laundry pink.
- The escape: Your catch slips free and vanishes through a tiny portal marked "Other Dryer."
- Rare catch: A matching pair of socks. The game treats this as a statistically impossible event.
- Sound: Watery gurgles, muffled spin cycles, and a satisfied dryer ding when you land a catch.

Same fishing mechanics, but finally an explanation for where your socks went.


Produced option: **"The Fridge at 3 AM"**

You're not fishing. You're standing in front of an open fridge, wearing one slipper, hunting for something that will fix your life.

- Environment: A vast refrigerated wilderness. Ketchup-bottle skyscrapers, a glacier of freezer burn, and a suspicious container nobody has opened since 2019.
- Fisherman: A raccoon in a bathrobe, fishing with a spaghetti strand and a fork.
- Your catches: Nervous sausages, migrating pizza slices, and a broccoli floret wearing a cheese disguise.
- Apex predator: Grandma's leftovers. Nobody knows what they are anymore. They have developed tactics.
- Bait: Shredded cheese. You periodically eat the bait yourself.
- Escape animation: Food falls behind the vegetable drawer. Officially, it no longer exists.
- Legendary catch: An avocado during its eleven-second window of ripeness.
- When you lose: The fridge light comes on. Your character freezes. Someone whispers, "Are you eating again?"

The score is measured in "just one more bite."

Produced option: **"The Internet Is Down"**

Inside your router, a tiny civilization is desperately pretending everything works.

- Environment: An overheated circuit-board desert. Ethernet cables grow like vines. A cooling fan spins above like a deeply unreliable sun.
- Fisherman: The offline dinosaur, sitting on a router and casting an Ethernet cable.
- Your catches: Feral browser tabs, buffering wheels, cookies with criminal records, and a VPN wearing a fake mustache.
- Bait: A giant green "DOWNLOAD" button. Everything knows it's a trap. Everything clicks anyway.
- Apex predator: A 47 GB update that approaches slowly, then stops at 99%.
- Escapes: Your catch splits into six pop-up windows. One starts playing music. Good luck finding which.
- Legendary catch: The actual "Unsubscribe" button.
- On a failed catch: A tiny technician appears and asks, "Have you tried turning it off and on again?"

Your score climbs steadily. The connection quality remains "Excellent. No Internet."

Produced option: **"Hell's Customer Support"** (selected for implementation)

Hell has frozen over. Not because of a prophecy - someone set the office thermostat to 16 degrees C. You're fishing souls out of the complaints department.

- Environment: A lava lake surrounded by cubicles. A flickering sign reads "Your suffering is important to us."
- Fisherman: A junior demon in a headset, using a pitchfork tied to a retractable ID badge.
- Your catches: Furious ghosts, possessed printers, and a man who's been demanding a manager since 1342.
- Bait: A sign saying "Speak to a real person."
- Apex predator: The manager. Three heads. None authorized to approve anything.
- Escapes: The soul shouts "TRANSFER ME!" and dives into another department.
- Legendary catch: A customer who says, "Actually, that solved my problem."
- Sound: Hold music played on a slightly out-of-tune recorder. Occasionally interrupted by "You are caller number... two million."

Every successful catch closes a ticket. Two more open.
